    Assumptions: lot size = (balance × risk%) ÷ (effective stop pips × pip value per standard lot), where effective stop = stop-loss + spread, rounded down to your lot step, with commission added on top so realized risk still lands on target. "Losers before DD" counts consecutive full-stop losses at fixed size and ignores anything beyond spread/commission (no slippage). Streak probability uses (1 − win rate)n as a simplified independent-Bernoulli estimate — it ignores any edge decay or correlation between trades. Kelly is the theoretical full-Kelly optimum (f* = W − (1−W)/R); most funded traders run at a fraction of it (¼–½ Kelly) because full Kelly has brutal variance. "Static" vs "trailing" DD only matters once winning trades occur — mid-losing-streak the two behave identically, since neither has moved the equity peak. This is a planning aid, not financial advice — verify every number against your own firm's actual rulebook before risking a real evaluation.
